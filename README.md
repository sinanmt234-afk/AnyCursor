# AnyCursor + AnyTrack
Control your Windows cursor with your head or hand, or with your phone as a trackpad.

**Head:** move head = move cursor · 1 blink = click · 2 blinks = double click · open mouth = right click
**Hand:** index finger moves cursor · thumb+index pinch = click/drag · thumb+middle = right click
**Pause/resume:** Ctrl+Alt+P

## Build (GitHub)
Push to `main` → Actions tab → download `AnyCursor-Windows` (run `AnyCursor.exe`) and `AnyTrack-Android` (install the APK).

## Phone
PC and phone on the same Wi-Fi. Open AnyTrack, enter the IP and PIN shown in the PC app. Allow AnyCursor through Windows Firewall (private network) when asked.
